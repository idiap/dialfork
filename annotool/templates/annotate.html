Before you start

You are helping check the quality of a research dataset of simulated customer-service conversations. There is no real personal data involved, and nothing about you is recorded beyond the answers you give and how long each one took.

You will read a conversation between an assistant and a user, and answer three short questions about whether the assistant followed the procedure it was given. It takes about 2–3 minutes per conversation. You can stop at any time and come back to the same link later.

Judge only the assistant. The user is played by another program and is never at fault. Whether the user ended up happy is not what we are asking about — only whether the assistant followed its own procedure.

Your answers will be used, in aggregate and anonymously, in a research paper.

3
The earlier part We highlight where the extra instruction started. Check whether the assistant follows its steps before that point, and leave a note if you like.

There are no trick scores and no rush — take the time you need. Only the assistant is judged; the user is just context.
